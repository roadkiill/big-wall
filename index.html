A final message from the creator

Floydian is shutting down.

Hey, I'm Onion, the creator of Floydian.

After a lot of thought, I've decided to shut Floydian down. The website has become increasingly expensive to keep running, especially with the amount of usage and the Firebase services needed to power it. Keeping everything online and maintained just isn't sustainable anymore.

Rather than keep the site running when I can no longer reasonably maintain it, I've decided it's time to close this chapter.

Looking for somewhere new to chat?
I've made PyChat, a new chatroom app built with Python. It's completely free to use, and you can create an account or continue as a guest.

Get PyChat

Thank you to everyone who used Floydian, made posts, chatted, and helped make this little community what it was. I genuinely appreciate it.

Goodbye, and thank you for everything.

— Roadkiill (Onion)
Creator of Floydian
